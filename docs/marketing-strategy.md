# Agora Marketing Strategy

## Positioning

Agora is open source project management for teams that want Asana-level clarity without ads, lock-in, or opaque AI.

The product is for teams that run real client or operational work and want a calmer command center they can inspect, export, self-host, and extend.

## Core Message

Project management software should organize work, not monetize attention or trap a team's operating system. Agora gives teams projects, clients, daily planning, automations, templates, time tracking, docs, approvals, and API-backed persistence while keeping the workspace portable and auditable.

## Taglines

- Open source project management without ads or lock-in.
- The self-hostable command center for serious team work.
- Project clarity you can own.
- Asana-style work management, open source infrastructure.
- Your workspace should not be an ad surface.

## Audience

Primary:

- Small agencies managing multiple clients.
- Consultants and operators who need company-scoped workspaces.
- Open source teams that want project management they can inspect.
- Privacy-conscious startups that do not want ads, trackers, or opaque AI in their operations layer.

Secondary:

- Internal operations teams coordinating projects, files, approvals, and time.
- Indie builders who want a self-hostable alternative to closed productivity suites.
- Nonprofits and community organizations that need templates, reporting, and auditability without vendor lock-in.

## Differentiators

- No ads, no trackers, no attention marketplace.
- Open source and self-hostable.
- Portable JSON, Markdown, and CSV export paths.
- Supabase-ready API storage without requiring a hosted SaaS account.
- Client/company views for teams managing more than one company.
- AI Operator with permissions, previews, rationale, audit logs, and undo.
- Project and automation template marketplace with creator pricing and charity payout metadata.
- Practical project management basics: boards, lists, calendar, daily planning, time, files, docs, approvals, reports, and notifications.

## Narrative Pillars

### 1. Own The Work

Teams should own their tasks, timelines, files, comments, templates, and automations. Agora's export and self-hosting path make portability a product promise, not a buried settings item.

### 2. Calm By Design

Modern teams do not need another noisy feed. Agora focuses on planning, delivery, client visibility, reporting, and recovery paths.

### 3. AI With Receipts

The Operator can help plan, draft, and recover work, but actions are governed by permissions and logged with rationale and undo context. This makes AI useful without making it mysterious.

### 4. Built For Client Work

Agencies, consultants, and multi-company project managers need scoped visibility, approvals, time tracking, templates, and reusable reports. Agora should speak directly to that workflow.

### 5. Open Marketplace

Templates and automations should be portable, remixable, and creator-friendly. Agora can make project operations shareable without forcing teams into a closed store.

## Competitor Framing

Do not position Agora as "all competitors are bad." The sharper claim is:

- Asana and Nifty are polished, but closed.
- ClickUp and monday are powerful, but can feel heavy.
- Notion is flexible, but teams often rebuild project management from scratch.
- Linear is elegant, but engineering-centric.
- Agora is for teams that want a project command center they can own, inspect, export, and shape.
